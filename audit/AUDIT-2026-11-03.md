## 1. Where Concat stands

**The engine got much faster.**

| What | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Enhance, per 1080p frame | 27 s | 4.2 s, on its own GPU shaders |
| Export, 4K60 footage at 4K | 11.3 s | 4.7 s, with the encoder on its own thread |
| Sound after a cut | Silent for seconds | Plays at once |
| Idle memory, 4K project | 590 MB | 511 MB |

A scrub now shows the nearest picture at once (#472).

Also on `main`, and in no release:
- Editing: linkage (captions and overlays follow ripples), a quick split
  that rides the playhead, Up/Down to jump between cuts, fade knobs on
  sound clips.
- Effects: a chroma key that finds the screen itself.
- Projects and export: transparent projects with ProRes 4444 or VP9 alpha
  export, and a background colour.
- Text: styled word ranges in titles.

**The licence story got simpler.** H.264, HEVC and AAC now go through the
platform's own encoders, and x264, x265, fdk-aac and espeak-ng are gone.
Every FFmpeg Concat ships is LGPL, and no GPL code is left in any build.
Concat is offered under the AGPL or a commercial licence, under a new CLA.

**`main` stopped being a signal and became a risk.**
- No CI run on `main` has passed since 24 October: 0 green in 54.
- The latest runs fail before compiling, because the workflows still
  fetch sherpa-onnx, which the code dropped the same night.
- A transition rewrite is committed as work in progress, with 16 tests
  deleted and none written.
- The phones' identifier changed, so the next phone build installs as a
  second, empty app.
- Each of these is fine as a step. Together, they mean `main` can't be
  released as it is, while 195 commits wait.

## 5. Open findings, High

- **N1. CI on `main` has been red for ten days (C-H1).**
  - Formatting drift fails every run, and clippy has failed since 26 October.
  - `cargo check` on macOS fails since 2 November.
  - Since `e63756d`, every engine and phone job dies on a sherpa-onnx
    download step that no longer has a crate to serve, and the models

  - Nobody knows whether `main` builds on a Mac or a phone.
- **N2. The #621 rewrite changes every saved transition and has no tests
  (X-H1).** Old transitions are re-centred on their cut, and a push over a
  held frame no longer becomes a dissolve. 16 transition tests were
  deleted, and the monitor/export parity test is ignored.
- **N3. The next phone build is a second app (C-H2).** Android and iOS
  treat `org.standalones.concat` as a new app. 80 000 phone downloads'
  projects, models and voices stay in the old one, and 0.2.8's updater
  will offer the new APK as an update.
- **O1, O2, O3, O4** are carried, as in section 4.

## 6. Open findings, Medium

- **Export.** HEVC is offered on every Windows machine, because Media
  Foundation counts as software and is never opened to check. Without
  Microsoft's HEVC extension there is likely no software HEVC encoder.
  With x265 gone, a stalled NVENC/QSV/AMF HEVC export has nothing to go on
  with on Windows or Linux (X-M1). H.264 on Windows N editions needs the
  same check.
- **Data.** M8, as above: one version bump would make 0.2.8 refuse these
  projects instead of eating them.
- **Licensing.** A commercial licence is offered while 0 of the 141
  clean-room items are done: 19 274 lines of outside code (D-M1). The
  notices describe removed components and leave out the bundled
  `bria.flac` (D-M2), whose licence is taken from a code repository's, not
  the recording's own (D-M3).
- **Phones.** No text to speech until the system voice is wired in, while
  the README promises it.
- **Triage.** 50 of the 70 issues opened since 24 October have no comment,
  including #533 and three GPU crashes on 0.2.8 (#556, #561, #600).
  Contributor fixes wait: #646, #620, #584, #582.
- **Carried:** E-M1, W-M7.
