## 1. Where Concat stands

**Reach keeps growing; trust does not.** v0.2.8 has 63 930 downloads in
four days: Windows 27 268 (setup 24 238, MSI 3 030), Android 21 397, iOS
4 472, macOS 3 095, Linux 2 639. 11 180 stars, 1 369 forks. Windows and
Android are 76% of it. Every one of those installs is unsigned, or
ad-hoc signed, and a new report (#533) shows the cost: Windows 11's Smart
App Control now refuses Concat's unsigned FFmpeg DLLs outright on a
machine where it ran a day earlier.

**The engine and the edit got a great deal better in four days.** Seven
format fixes (camera MTS/M2TS, MPEG-PS, HDV/DV pixel aspect, millisecond
timestamps in MKV/WebM/FLV/WMV, variable-rate screen recordings, damaged
packets, one time origin per file); captions timed by voice detection
instead of 1-7 s early; posters in about 0.1-0.2 s instead of up to 17 s;
a hardware encoder that stalls mid-export now finishes on the CPU;
effects and sound effects are layers on the timeline; copy and paste
carry every clip field; preferences are written atomically and privately.

**The shell keeps absorbing it.** `studio.rs` is 13 180 lines (12 739 on
20 October), `start()` 2 341 (2 240), the export's `lib.rs` 4 358
(3 770). No file was split. The Compose spike showed why it matters: a
native phone shell would have to re-implement the editing rules that live
in `studio.rs`, because no UI-agnostic controller exists.

**`main` gave no signal again.** CI on `main` was red from 23 October
04:16 to 24 October (rustfmt drift, a locale key, three clippy lints),
and almost every other run was cancelled by a newer push. Fixed in
`e854f82`; the habit that caused it - pushing without the seconds-long
local gates - is the finding.

## 5. Open findings, High

- **O1. Windows installs are refused (#533, distribution).** Smart App
  Control blocks the unsigned BtbN FFmpeg DLLs, on machines where 0.2.7
  ran. Windows is 44% of downloads. SignPath Foundation will not sign
  upstream binaries; the ways out are the Microsoft Store (the
  `jub0t.Concat` identity is reserved) or FFmpeg and sherpa-onnx built in
  our own CI and signed with the rest.
- **O2. The update manifest is unsigned (H5).** It is now fetched over the
  shipped roots alone, which closes today's regression, but anyone who can
  publish a release can still ship any installer. A compiled-in key and a
  signature on the manifest close it.
- **O3. Export recovery takes the road that crashes hybrid laptops (H7).**
  Unchanged since 15 October.
- **O4. 117 commits reach nobody.** The macOS 12/13 launch crash (#532),
  the WARP crashes (#503, #509, #523), the Android encoder stall (#491)
  and every fix above are on `main` only, and every report about them
  will keep coming until a release carries them.

## 6. Open findings, Medium

Full evidence in the area reports.

- **Engine.** The playback reader's slack is counted in source frames, so
  60-240 fps clips, speed ramps and 1080i still open two or three readers
  (E-M1). Sound seeks never check where they landed, so a TS/PS file
  trimmed from the front can play silence at its head (E-M2). The phone's
  software rerun goes to `c2.android.*` encoders, which likely cap below 4K
  and at 8 bits, so a stalled 4K or 10-bit export renders twice and fails
  (E-M3, unverified). No deinterlacer: 1080i shows combing. TS scrubbing
  ~40 ms a frame against ~15 for MP4.
- **Data.** A paste into another project carries the source's media ids
  in Enhance and in image backgrounds (W-M5, M6). 0.2.8 opening a project
  with a sound layer drops it, and its next autosave deletes it (M8): a
  project format version that older builds refuse would prevent it.
- **Window.** A card's fold is remembered by row, so it moves with a
  reorder (W-M2). Every return to the window checks every media file on
  the UI thread, a freeze on Windows network drives (W-M7). Ctrl+W, Ctrl+O
  and Ctrl+Q still fire from a text field. Keyboard access to the new
  switch, pills and fold, and the "eye" in the chain's help text, in all 26
  languages, are Low.
- **Server and agents.** Unchanged since 20 October: one shared auth
  throttle bucket, agent-opened projects locked until the server ends, the
  project lock opening on any I/O error, `concat --mcp` never run in CI, no
  size caps on downloads, phone FFmpeg and sherpa pulled without digests,
  the model mirror 404.
- **Phones.** The toast sits under the navigation bar; no soft-keyboard
  inset, RTL or accessibility names. The Compose spike is a workspace
  member, so every CI job builds and links it.
- **Speech.** The VAD step cannot be cancelled and shows no progress.

## 8. What to do, in order

1. **Release from a green `main`.** Let one full CI run and one phone run
   finish on the tagged commit. The crash fixes above reach nobody before.
2. **Choose the #533 road** - Microsoft Store submission, or FFmpeg and
   sherpa-onnx built and signed in CI - and say plainly in the release
   notes and on #533 that Smart App Control blocks the current build.
3. **Sign the update manifest** with a key compiled into the app.
4. **Register as an Android developer** for `app.concat.editor` before
   Google's verification rollout.
5. **Extract the editor controller from `studio.rs`** into a crate with no
   Slint in it: it is the maintainability score's only real fix, it makes
   the inspector testable without a window, and the native phone shell
   needs it. The spike's estimate: 4-6 weeks.
6. **The Mediums:** E-M2 (check sound seeks), E-M1 (slack in output
   frames), M6 (remap Enhance and backgrounds on paste), a project format
   version (M8), W-M2, W-M7.
7. **Close the 55 issues the sweep found done or duplicated** (a script is
   ready) and correct the two false lines in the 0.2.8 notes.
8. **Before every push:** `cargo fmt`, the locale check and clippy on the
   crates touched. Seconds, and they keep `main` a signal.
