# Concat audit, 11 October 2026

Where the codebase stands two days after the 9 October audit, at
`7223d64` (10 October), 71 commits past the 0.2.6 tag and 8 commits past
the last audit. Same method, same scales, every finding from the 9th (and
what was carried from the 4th and the 28th) re-verified against today's
tree. Every finding names a file and a line. The sibling
`AUDIT-2026-10-11-QUICK.md` is this audit as a one-screen scorecard.

**How it was done.** Four reviewers each read one part of the tree in
full: the window's Rust, Slint and locales; host, server, API and CLI;
media, render, export and core; project, effects, text, vision, speech,
Android, perf, CI and release. Each re-verified the 9 October findings in
its part against today's lines, read the commits since the audit as
commits (does each do what its message says, with what test, missing
what), scored its part and reported new findings with locations. The
headline items (the Windows job still timing out after the fix that was
meant to free it; the three dialogs still opened under the studio borrow;
the paste that does not keep its shape) were re-read by hand against the
code and, for CI, against the workflow runs through GitHub's API. Line,
test and `unsafe` tallies come from the tree by the same counting as the
9th (Rust lines are `src/` only, without `build.rs` and `tests/`
directories; the unwrap column's bracket is the count outside test
modules). `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets`,
`cargo test --workspace`, `scripts/locales.py --check`,
`scripts/models.py --check` and `cargo run -p concat-perf --release --
--check` were run on this machine (Windows 10, x86_64, FFmpeg n8.1 shared
build) today. The app itself was not launched; no phone build was made.

**What the eight commits are.** All on 9 and 10 October, six by the
maintainer and one by a contributor merged on the 10th: the 9 October
audit itself (48e48a1); the release pipeline's three holes closed
(3f3b7de: a release builds its tag, Android is signed on release runs
alone, the cards test skips on a software adapter, the toolchain is read
from the pin); the data-loss class closed (a2dd5e3: an atomic mux, one
failure policy for the export's two decode paths, `MAX_SIDE` and
`MAX_TIME` as the ceilings a document is held to, uncaptured-error
handlers on both devices); the export sheet made careful (9a2e5f2: asks
before replacing, refuses a name that is not a file's, dialogs through a
new `host::after_dialog`); copy and paste of a selection across timelines
as one undo step (c260c2d, #290); the meters pane as one instrument
(948b6db); and the add-track menu's rows taking their clicks (441544e,
#296). The first three took the 9 October audit's steps 1 to 3 the same
day. The reviewers judged all seven; section 6 is largely about them.

**Scale.** 0 to 10. 8 and up means "keep doing this"; 5 to 7 means
"works, with known debt"; 4 and under means "will bite at the next order
of magnitude, or already bites".

## 9. Caveats

Scores are judgements over code, calibrated against the 9 October scores
but not measured against users or another editor. Every crate was read in
full by one of four reviewers; the headline findings were re-read by hand.
Findings marked (verified) were read at the line and, where a command
could show it, run; (reasoned) means the path was followed through the
code and not executed. The checks ran on a Windows machine with a
discrete GPU, which is not the runner's WARP, so the CI hang could not be
reproduced here and was read from the workflow runs' annotations only.
The app was not launched; no phone build was made; Windows text at 100 %
and the phone's export remain unseen on the hardware they are for.
`cargo audit` was not run.
