The free, open-source CapCut replacement.

Concat does what you already use CapCut for — multi-track editing, captions, voice tools, templates — with no watermarks, no account, and nothing behind a subscription. A native Rust engine runs it all on your own machine.

How it stacks up

Against CapCut, the app it replaces, and OpenCut, the other open-source alternative.

FeatureConcatCapCutOpenCutNotes
PerformanceConcat and CapCut are native. OpenCut runs on WebAssembly FFmpeg in a browser.
PriceCapCut is free until Pro effects, 4K, or AI tools, then $9.99–$19.99 a month.
WatermarkCapCut stamps exports that use Pro assets.
PrivacyConcat sends nothing anywhere. CapCut's terms grant a perpetual licence to your uploads.
OfflineConcat's captions, speech, cutout, and export all run on-device. CapCut's best features need the cloud.
Open sourceConcat is AGPL, OpenCut is MIT, CapCut is closed source.
4K exportCapCut caps free exports at 1080p. OpenCut depends on the browser.
Effects & templatesCapCut has thousands. Concat has a few dozen. OpenCut has a basic set.
AI toolsCapCut has tracking, reframe, and avatars. Concat has local captions, speech, and person cutout.
KeyframesConcat keys position, scale, rotation, and opacity with bezier easing — no curve editor yet, and effect parameters aren't keyable.
Export formatsConcat writes H.264 MP4 only. OpenCut supports MP4 and WebM.
StabilityConcat is a 0.2.x beta. OpenCut is mid rewrite.
MobileConcat's Android and iOS builds compile but are untested. OpenCut's are in progress.
ExtensibilityOpenCut ships an Editor API, MCP server, and plugins. Concat's plugin API is planned.
CommunityOpenCut has tens of thousands of stars. Concat has a Discord and a handful of contributors.
Multiple timelines per projectConcat only.
Strong Partial, or with strings attached Weak or missing

v0.2.1

What's new

  • Android and iOS builds are now part of every nightly and release, not a separate process.
  • Export now matches your project's own aspect ratio instead of a fixed default.
  • The release APK is signed.
  • Media types are colour-coded from the brand palette in the interface.
  • A pass through the codebase turned up and fixed a handful of bugs.
01

On macOS, clear quarantine

Builds are unsigned, so macOS blocks them by default. Run this once after installing:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Concat.app
02

Start cutting

No account, no extra downloads, no setup. Open Concat and import your first clip.
